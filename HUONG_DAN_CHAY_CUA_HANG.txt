FASTOR Store + Admin – ASP.NET MVC5 / EF6 / SQL Server

MO TRONG VISUAL STUDIO 2026
1. Giai nen ZIP ra thu muc MOI (khong ghi de project cu dang chay).
2. Mo KtraBE.sln, Restore NuGet neu duoc hoi, sau do nhan F5.
3. Truy cap https://localhost:PORT/ (trang ban hang; port theo IIS Express cua ban).
4. Admin: https://localhost:PORT/Account/Login (admin / Admin@2026), sau do /Admin/Orders.
5. Customer: vao /CustomerAccount/Register de tao TAI KHOAN MOI, dung tai khoan nay de dang nhap, them vao gio, dat hang (tien mat / chuyen khoan demo), xem lich su mua.

DATABASE
- Dung chung database MyStore_BE_Ktra vua tao. Khong can chay lai SQL neu 31 don hang mau da co.
- File Web.config: Data Source=(localdb)\MSSQLLocalDB;Initial Catalog=MyStore_BE_Ktra.
- Tai khoan khach hang mau khach01...khach08 co Password=NOLOGIN chi de gan du lieu mau; hay tao tai khoan khach hang moi de thu nghiem.

KHA NANG
- Admin: danh sach, tim kiem, loc, phan trang 15 don/trang, xem hoa don, cap nhat trang thai.
- Customer: danh sach san pham theo danh muc, tim kiem, chi tiet san pham, gio hang Session, dang ky/nhap, dat hang ghi vao SQL, lich su mua.
- Thanh toan chi mo phong, khong co PayPal / cong thanh toan that.

LUU Y
- Day la ma nguon de mo thu va chay tren Windows; chua chay end-to-end IIS Express tren may cua ban duoc.
- Neu mat trang trong: thu https://localhost:PORT/Home/Index.
